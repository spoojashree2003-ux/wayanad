A Little Adventure
Awaits

Nestled in the Western Ghats at an altitude of 700–2100 metres, Wayanad is Kerala's most enchanting secret. A land where ancient caves hold prehistoric stories, where forests whisper with wildlife, and where every sunrise paints the mist gold.

Whether you seek the thrill of a mountain summit, the serenity of a hidden waterfall, the warmth of tribal culture, or simply the joy of breathing clean mountain air — Wayanad delivers it all, effortlessly.

Must-Visit Places

Places to Explore

Six iconic landmarks that define the soul of Wayanad

Edakkal Caves

Ancient cave etchings from the Neolithic era. A window into 6,000 years of history, perched on a rocky hill.

🔮 History & Heritage

Soochipara Waterfalls

A magnificent three-tiered waterfall plunging 200 feet into a crystal-clear pool in the heart of the forest.

💦 Waterfalls

Chembra Peak

The highest peak in Wayanad at 2100m. Home to a heart-shaped lake and the most rewarding trek in Kerala.

⛰️ Trekking

Banasura Sagar Dam

India's largest earthen dam. A serene reservoir surrounded by the Banasura Hills, perfect for boating and nature walks.

🚤 Boating & Scenic

Wayanad Wildlife Sanctuary

A biodiverse haven for elephants, tigers, leopards, and over 200 bird species across 344 sq km of protected forest.

🐘 Wildlife Safari

Kuruva Island

A cluster of protected river islands accessed only by bamboo rafts. A pristine ecological paradise with rare birds and plants.

🏝️ River & Nature
